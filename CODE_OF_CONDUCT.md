# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment
include:

- **Using welcoming and inclusive language**
- **Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences**
- **Gracefully accepting constructive criticism**
- **Focusing on what is best for the community**
- **Showing empathy towards other community members**
- **Being patient and understanding with newcomers**
- **Encouraging collaboration and knowledge sharing**
- **Giving credit where credit is due**
- **Helping others learn and grow**

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment, bullying, or discrimination**
- **Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments**
- **Personal attacks or political discussions**
- **Public or private harassment**
- **Publishing private information without permission**
- **Spamming or self-promotion without value**
- **Deliberate intimidation or threats**
- **Creating a hostile or unwelcoming environment**
- **Disruptive behavior during meetings or discussions**
- **Abuse of power or authority**

## Our Responsibilities

### Project Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions
that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or
permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### Community Members

Community members are responsible for:

- **Reading and following this Code of Conduct**
- **Reporting inappropriate behavior to maintainers**
- **Helping newcomers understand community norms**
- **Treating all members with respect and kindness**
- **Focusing on constructive communication**
- **Assuming good faith in interactions**

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the NeuroSleepNet project,
including but not limited to:

- **GitHub repositories** (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- **Discord community server**
- **Email communications**
- **Meetings and conferences**
- **Social media channels**
- **Documentation and code reviews**

## Enforcement

### Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through one of
these channels:

**For Immediate Issues:**
- Email: conduct@neurosleepnet.dev
- Discord: Direct message to any maintainer

**For Non-Urgent Issues:**
- GitHub: Report an issue with the "Code of Conduct" label
- Email: conduct@neurosleepnet.dev

### What to Include in Your Report

Please include as much information as possible:

- **Your contact information** (if you're comfortable sharing)
- **Description of the incident** (what happened, when, where)
- **Individuals involved** (if known)
- **Any witnesses** (if applicable)
- **Screenshots or logs** (if available)
- **Desired outcome** (what you'd like to see happen)

### Response Timeline

We will respond to all reports within 48 hours and provide a timeline for
resolution. All reports will be handled confidentially.

### Resolution Process

1. **Initial Response**: Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
2. **Investigation**: Review the incident thoroughly
3. **Action**: Take appropriate corrective action
4. **Follow-up**: Ensure resolution is effective
5. **Documentation**: Update policies if needed

### Possible Actions

Depending on the severity of the violation, actions may include:

- **Warning**: Private message explaining the issue
- **Temporary Ban**: Removal from community for a set period
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from all community spaces
- **Content Removal**: Deletion of inappropriate content
- **Public Statement**: Communication about serious violations

## Additional Guidelines

### Communication Standards

- **Assume good faith**: Give others the benefit of the doubt
- **Be constructive**: Focus on solutions, not just problems
- **Stay on topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the project
- **Use appropriate channels**: Choose the right place for your message
- **Respect privacy**: Don't share private conversations publicly

### Code Review Standards

- **Be thorough but kind**: Review code carefully, but be polite
- **Focus on the code, not the person**: Critique the implementation, not the author
- **Provide actionable feedback**: Suggest specific improvements
- **Acknowledge good work**: Recognize quality contributions
- **Be patient with newcomers**: Help them learn the process

### Meeting Standards

- **Be prepared**: Read materials in advance
- **Stay on topic**: Keep discussions focused
- **Respect time limits**: Be mindful of others' schedules
- **Listen actively**: Pay attention to different perspectives
- **Participate constructively**: Add value to discussions

## Questions or Concerns

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct or need clarification on any
aspect, please contact:

- **Email**: conduct@neurosleepnet.dev
- **GitHub**: Open an issue with the "Code of Conduct" label

We are committed to making NeuroSleepNet a welcoming and inclusive community for
everyone. Your help in maintaining this environment is greatly appreciated.
